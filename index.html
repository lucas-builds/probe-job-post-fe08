Probes adapt to the role, not the other way around

Every probe is tuned to the specific skills a role requires, adjusting difficulty as a candidate answers — so a senior backend role and a junior frontend role are never measured on the same scale.

  • Difficulty recalibrates in real time per candidate
  • Built around the actual tools and constraints of the role
  • Results come back as a clear pass signal, not a raw score to interpret
